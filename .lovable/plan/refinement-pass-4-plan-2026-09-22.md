# Refinement Pass 4 Plan

## Direction

Apply the selected **Editorial personal brand journey** direction to the existing site. Keep the current Caslon/Comfortaa typography, hot-pink identity, charcoal contrast, white-led canvas, approved copy, routes, and architecture. Use the selected concept for composition—not its placeholder text, fonts, or invented details.

## What will change

1. **Global visual rhythm**
   - Tighten the type scale so large screens feel editorial rather than oversized.
   - Standardize section spacing, readable line lengths, border radius, shadows, and CTA hierarchy.
   - Keep white and soft off-white dominant; use blush and warm beige only as accents.

2. **Editorial doodle language**
   - Build reusable, lightweight hand-drawn marks from local inline shapes: underlines, arrows, scribbles, notes, paper planes, books, pens, roots, checks, and bank-alert motifs.
   - Use faint marginalia around headings and open whitespace.
   - Add only a few larger editorial compositions for Start Here, Work With Me, Books/Resources, and Good Mornings With Kuku.
   - Keep decorative artwork hidden from assistive technology and reduce or remove it on narrow screens where it could interfere.

3. **Core page composition**
   - Preserve the homepage portrait as the primary photographic moment and keep the short introduction text-led.
   - Make Start Here pathways feel like an annotated routing page rather than six identical product cards.
   - Differentiate the Work With Me offers, balance the $120 price, and retain emphasis on “Bring the problem. Let’s find the move.”
   - Present both books as one authored body of work while keeping Dear Bank Alert as the current focus.
   - Make Writing feel like a notebook/writing room and Resources like an open personal library.
   - Keep Reader Stories complete without fabricated stories; simplify Partnerships and Contact forms visually.

4. **Responsive and accessibility corrections**
   - Fix the confirmed 320px overflow on Home, Start Here, and Books.
   - Test 320px, 375px, 390px, 430px, and wide desktop across all major public pages.
   - Improve mobile heading wrapping, button sizing, form grouping, menu touch targets, image height, and footer readability.
   - Add accessible grouping and explanatory status text for unavailable form actions; remove the misleading always-visible contact success announcement.
   - Retain visible keyboard focus, semantic headings, image alternatives, and reduced-motion behavior.

5. **Copy, CTA, and metadata audit**
   - Preserve all approved client copy and emojis.
   - Replace only accidental generic or inconsistent CTA labels where an approved label already exists.
   - Keep Good Mornings With Kuku naming consistent.
   - Correct remaining public metadata that describes pages as placeholders or future shells, without inventing claims.
   - Leave the client-supplied Dear Bank Alert sentence containing “becoming” unchanged.

## Verification

- Compare every major public page side by side at desktop and mobile widths.
- Verify desktop navigation remains fully visible and mobile navigation remains usable.
- Check overflow, clipping, button wrapping, forms, image rhythm, doodle collisions, contrast, focus behavior, and footer composition.
- Confirm page metadata, approved CTA labels, first-person voice, emojis, and clean diagnostics.
